The path

Four stages, and you always know which one you are in.

No black boxes and no silent months. Every stage ends with something in your hands and a decision that is yours to make.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    Thirty minutes on your book, your deadline, and your budget. You leave with a recommendation — including "you don't need us for this yet" when that is the honest answer.

  2. 02

    Scope and sample

    A fixed scope, a fixed price, and a free sample — 1,000 edited words, or a sample chapter for ghostwriting — before you commit to anything.

  3. 03

    Write, edit, design

    Work arrives in agreed milestones with your project manager on every message. Revisions are built into the quote rather than billed as extras afterwards.

  4. 04

    Publish and hand over

    Files, ISBNs, retailer listings, and account access transferred to you. The accounts are in your name from day one, so nothing is ever held hostage.

Why authors stay

A publisher takes your rights and pays you a royalty. We take a fee and give you the book.

That difference decides everything else: who controls the price, who owns the file, who can move the book to another platform in five years, and who gets paid when it sells at three in the morning in a country you have never visited.

Three promises

You own everything

Copyright, byline, ISBNs, cover files, retailer accounts, and 100% of royalties. We are a service you hire, not a publisher you sign to. It appears in the contract in plain English, not in a schedule at the back.

One person, start to finish

A named project manager who knows your book, answers your emails, and stays until it ships. No ticket queues, and no being re-introduced to your own manuscript by a stranger in month four.

The price is the price

Scope and cost are agreed before work starts, revisions included. If scope genuinely changes we tell you before touching it — never afterwards, on an invoice, when the book is already half-finished.
